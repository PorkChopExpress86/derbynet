# Quick reference

Where to find each task in DerbyNet. Each line reads "page, then the button or control to use". Quoted words are the labels on the screen.

## Find your way around

- On a computer, the menu is the sidebar on the left, in four parts: Before, During, After, and Other.
- On a phone, the bar at the bottom holds three pages for your role, and "More" holds the rest. A race coordinator gets Dashboard, Check-In and Standings. The race crew gets Check-In, Photos and On Deck.
- Settings, racing groups, the rounds playlist, the imports, roles and passwords, and events open from Set-Up, under "Before race day".
- "Quick reference" at the bottom of the menu, or of "More" on a phone, opens this page. Every guide is part of the server, so they work with no internet.
- "Den" below stands for your partition label, which you may have renamed.

## Events and data

- Start the first event: open DerbyNet. A new server opens Set-Up, at "Create or open an event".
- New event: Set-Up, then "Events: copy, switch, archive, export", then "New event". Choose what to copy under "What to copy". Racers, results and photos are never copied (D14).
- Switch to another event: Events, then "Make active" on its card. A past event is read-only until "Reopen for editing" (D81).
- Archive an event: Events, then "Archive".
- Export an event to move it: Events, then "Export zip".
- Delete results, schedules, racers or awards: Set-Up, then "Data", then "Delete race results…", "Delete schedules…", "Delete racers…" or "Delete awards…". The server saves a snapshot first (D78).
- Start the event over: Set-Up, then "Data", then "Re-initialize…".
- Download a snapshot: Set-Up, then "Snapshots", then "Snapshot, complete".
- Import a snapshot: Set-Up, then "Snapshots", then "Snapshot to import", then "Import snapshot…". It replaces all the event's data.
- Make a fake roster to practice: Set-Up, then "Before race day", then "Fake roster".

## Settings and rules

- Drop each racer's slowest heat: Set-Up, then "Settings", then "Rules", then "Scoring": "Drop slowest".
- Interleave heats of different groups: Settings, then "Rules", then "Interleave heats from different groups".
- Race by points instead of times: Settings, then "Rules", then "Race by points".
- Number of lanes: Settings, then "Track", then "Lanes on the track". It is locked while heats are left to race.
- Lanes not in use: Settings, then "Track", then "Lanes in use". Or the rounds playlist, under "Scheduling method and lanes".
- Lane colors: Settings, then "Track", then "Lane colors".
- Time precision: Settings, then "Track", then "Time precision".
- Weigh-in at check-in: Settings, then "Weigh-in", per den.
- Folders for photos and videos: Settings, then "Photos and video", then type a folder under the data folder, and press "Check".
- Change a password: Set-Up, then "Roles and passwords". See [Roles and passwords](roles-and-passwords.md).

## Track and timer

- The timer's status: on a phone, Dashboard, then "Timer status". The timer page is at `/timer/`.
- Connect the timer through the browser: on the computer the timer is plugged into, the timer page, then "Connect a timer in this browser". It needs Chrome or Edge, and an HTTPS address (see [Secure contexts](secure-contexts.md)).
- Test the timer: the timer page, then "Timer test".
- Race with no timer: Settings, then "Track", then turn off "Warn when no timer is connected". Then, for each heat, Dashboard, then "Manual results", type the times or places, and press "Save results".
- Start the race from the dashboard, with a remote start gate: Dashboard, then "Start Race".

## Racers and check-in

- Import the roster: Set-Up, then "Import roster". Choose the file, match the columns, then "Preview" and "Import".
- Add a racer: Race Check-In, then "New racer", then "Add racer".
- Change a racer: Race Check-In, then tap the racer's name, change the fields, and press "Save".
- Delete a racer: Race Check-In, then tap the name, then "Delete racer". A racer already in a schedule cannot be deleted.
- Check a racer in: Race Check-In, then the "Passed" switch on the racer's card.
- Check in a whole den: Race Check-In, then "Bulk", choose the den under "Racers", then "Check in" and "Apply".
- Renumber racers: Race Check-In, then "Bulk", then "Renumber", then "Apply".
- Make a den not eligible for awards: Race Check-In, then "Bulk", choose the den, then "Award eligibility", then "Not eligible for any award".
- Add a late racer to a round: the racer's card, then "Add to round…". Or Dashboard, then "Not in the schedule", then "Preview and add".
- Check in from a second phone: Race Check-In, then "Phone QR", and scan the code with the other phone. The code works for 60 minutes.

## Groups

- Choose how racers are grouped: Set-Up, then "Racing groups", then "How racers are grouped": by den, one group, or custom.
- Add a den: Racing groups, then the dens button, then "New".
- Add a group, under the custom rule: Racing groups, then the plus "Group" button.
- Add an aggregate group: Racing groups, then the plus "Aggregate" button.
- Rename a group or subgroup: Racing groups, then tap its name.
- Reorder groups or subgroups: Racing groups, then drag the handle, or the row menu, then "Move up" or "Move down".
- Speed trophies for one group: Racing groups, then tap the group, then "Speed trophies".
- Use subgroups: Racing groups, then "Labels", then "Use subgroups". Subgroups show under the one-group and custom rules (D50).
- Delete a group: move or delete its racers first, then Racing groups, tap the group, and "Delete".

## Rounds and the playlist

- Plan the race day: Set-Up, then "Rounds playlist". Tap each round under "Rounds to add", set "Runs per lane" and "And then", and press "Add to playlist". "Wait for operator" stops between rounds for an intermission (D12).
- Run the playlist: Rounds playlist, then "Start playlist". Or Dashboard, then "Start Playlist".
- After an intermission: Dashboard, then "Start next round".
- Make a final or another round: Dashboard, then "Add New Rounds".
- Schedule a round: Dashboard, then "Schedule" on the round's card, then "Schedule Only" or "Schedule + Race".
- Race a round: Dashboard, then "Race" on the round's card.
- Unschedule a round before it races: Dashboard, then "Unschedule".
- Run a round again: Dashboard, then "Repeat Round". It deletes the round's results.
- Delete a later round: Dashboard, then "Delete Round". A first round goes with its group.

## Heats

- Move to another heat: Dashboard, then "Previous" or "Next heat" on the heat card.
- Turn racing on or off: Dashboard, the switch on the heat card.
- Run a heat again: Dashboard, then "Re-Run This Heat". "Reinstate Heat" undoes it.
- Discard a heat's results: make it the current heat, then "Manual results", then "Discard results".
- Close a damaged lane: Dashboard, then "Close a lane".

## Awards

- Import awards: Set-Up, then "Import awards".
- Speed trophies: Settings, then "Awards". Or Racing groups, then "Speed trophies".
- Add an award: Judging, then "Edit awards", then "New award".
- Change or delete an award: Awards editor, then tap the award.
- Give an award: Judging, then tap the award, then tap the racer.
- Take an award back: Judging, then the "X" beside the winner's name on the award.
- Let the audience vote: Judging, then "Manage ballot", then "Balloting is open". Each racer's card at check-in has a "Voting code".
- Present awards: Kiosk Dashboard, set a kiosk's "Page" to "Award presentations". Then Present Awards, tap the award, and press "Reveal".

## Kiosks

- Name a kiosk: Kiosk Dashboard, then the kiosk's "Name".
- Change what a kiosk shows: Kiosk Dashboard, then the kiosk's "Page".
- Switch every kiosk at once: Kiosk Dashboard, then "Scene".
- Edit the scenes: Kiosk Dashboard, then "Scene editor".

## Photos

- Take a photo at check-in: Race Check-In, then tap the racer's photo or car tile, then "Take racer photo" or "Take car photo".
- Upload many photos: Racer Photos or Car Photos, then "Upload photos".
- Crop or turn a photo: Racer Photos or Car Photos, then tap the photo.
- Give a photo to a racer: Racer Photos or Car Photos, then tap the photo under "Unassigned photos", then tap the racer.
- Take a photo away from a racer: Racer Photos or Car Photos, then "Unassign" on the racer's card.

## Replay

- Set up the replay station: log in, then "Replay Kiosk" on the login page. Choose the camera, then "Proceed".
- Use a phone as the replay camera: "Be a Camera" on the login page.
- Test replay: Dashboard, then "Trigger Replay".

## After the race

- Standings: Standings.
- Export results: Export Results, then a spreadsheet or JSON file.
- Import results: Set-Up, then "Import roster", then the "Results" tab.
